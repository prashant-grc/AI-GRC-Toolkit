# AI Fraud Investigation Toolkit

The AI Fraud Investigation Toolkit is an open-source resource created by CyberSecureHub to support fraud detection, investigation workflows and digital trust using emerging AI technologies.

## Purpose

Fraud is becoming faster, more automated and increasingly AI-enabled. This toolkit provides practical resources for understanding and investigating:

- Identity fraud
- Payment fraud
- Deepfake-enabled fraud
- Social engineering
- Mule account activity
- Insider-assisted fraud
- AI-supported investigation workflows

## Contents

- Fraud Investigation Checklist
- AI Fraud Risk Matrix
- Digital Evidence Collection Template
- Fraud Triage Workflow
- Investigation Reporting Template

## Target Audience

- Fraud investigators
- Cybersecurity professionals
- Financial crime teams
- GRC teams
- Banks, fintechs and insurers
- Digital trust professionals

## Status

Initial public release. More templates and investigation workflows will be added progressively.
